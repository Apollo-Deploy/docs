## What you can do

- **Progressive rollouts** — Stage canary, limited, and full rollouts with health gates.
- **Signal correlation** — Connect Sentry, App Store vitals, and deploy events in one view.
- **Release risk** — Spot regressions and abort bad rollouts before they reach everyone.
